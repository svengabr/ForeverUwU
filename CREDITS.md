# Credits

## Sounds

The voice clips are popular anime and meme sound bites as shared on [myinstants.com](https://www.myinstants.com),
trimmed, cut to 2.5 s, made mono and leveled to the same loudness for the addon. All rights stay with their
owners; if you own one of these clips and want it removed, open an issue on GitHub and it will be taken out.

## Font

The floating text uses [Mochiy Pop One](https://github.com/fontdasu/Mochiypop) by the Mochiypop Project Authors,
licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](fonts/OFL.txt). The addon ships a copy cut down to Latin characters.
