# Credits

## Sounds

The voice clips are popular anime and meme sound bites as shared on [myinstants.com](https://www.myinstants.com) and
YouTube, trimmed, cut to 2.5 s, made mono and leveled to the same loudness for the addon. All rights stay with their
owners; if you own one of these clips and want it removed, open an issue on GitHub and it will be taken out.

Clips cut from YouTube: Hannah OwO, Pokimane, Ironmouse, Gawr Gura, Kobo Kanaeru, Neuro-sama, Kureiji Ollie,
"40 VTubers saying Ara Ara", Love Live! (Nico Nico Nii), Steins;Gate (Tuturu), Non Non Biyori (Nyanpasu),
Demon Slayer (Shinobu), Kaguya-sama (Chika) and uwu / ara ara meme compilations.
